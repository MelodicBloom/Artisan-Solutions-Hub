## Repository-specific records

### D25 — Artisan Solutions Hub: outcome-based home-service UX

**Use when:** When recovering Artisan Solutions Hub, its contractor-service case study, package/pricing flows, visualizer, or lead/estimate journey.

[Recovered identity] The generic README describes an artisan marketplace, but replit.md identifies a specific A-Team Repair Solutions customer site for Philadelphia-area installation/repair. Do not conflate those product contexts. The specific workflow deserves recovery before a generalized marketplace expansion.

[Design DNA] Gold #C9A84C, charcoal #111113/#1C1C1E, cream #FAF8F4, Playfair display and Open Sans reading type. The product groups services by outcomes—moving day, home office/workout space, entertainment/sound, bathroom, kitchen, basement—then offers a detailed catalog, before/after gallery, Show My Space lead capture, estimate scheduling, articles, and an AI concept visualizer.

[Observed boundary] Server/database code exists and names DATABASE_URL plus Replit-scoped OpenAI integration variables. The context document's “verified pairs,” accessibility, bookings, and SEO claims are not independently verified in this pass. A record in an estimate table does not prove an actual calendar meeting.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Resolve the product charter and intended audience. Separate genuine project evidence, single-image showcases, concept renders, seeded testimonials, submitted requests, and confirmed appointments. Preserve a useful non-AI path. An AI room visualization is concept exploration, not a construction scope or estimate.

[Reimagined horizon] A trustworthy contractor/client workspace with a clear service-selection and evidence journey, potentially informing the later reusable service platform.

[Carry-forward test] One package leads to a correctly prefilled inquiry/estimate request, shows actual submission state, preserves privacy, and remains usable without generated imagery. Case-study claims map to artifacts and observed prototype behavior.
